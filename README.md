2. Store Detailed Description (English)
Boost your Amazon sales with the ultimate, free listing audit tool.
Are you losing Amazon search visibility and sales due to incomplete listings? The Amazon Listing Health & CDQ Analyzer is a lightweight, professional-grade Chrome extension that instantly audits any Amazon product listing globally. Get actionable suggestions to optimize your content, satisfy Amazon's strict algorithms, and maximize shopper conversion.

Key Capabilities:
🏆 Instant Listing Health Score (0-100): Understand how well your listing aligns with Amazon's official retail best practices.
🛡️ CDQ (Composite Data Quality) Audit: Amazon evaluates listings on CDQ completeness to qualify ASINs for Prime Day deals, Lightning Deals, and search boosts. This extension checks for:
Basic completeness: Title, Brand, Category, Bullet points, Description.
Golden Core Attributes: Material, Capacity, Size/Dimensions, Weight, and Color.
Key Characteristics: Connector interfaces/power/wattage (for Electronics) or sizing maps/target gender (for Fashion).
📝 2025 Title Repetition Rule Validation: Protect your listings from search suppression! We instantly check if any key descriptive terms are repeated more than twice (as per the strict January 21, 2025 update).
🔍 Image Zoom & Compliance Checker:
Checks if your gallery contains at least 4 images.
Analyzes the high-resolution master asset to confirm if the longest side is >= 1,600px to activate Amazon's crucial hover-zoom function.
Performs off-screen canvas color-pixel analysis to verify if the main image's background is pure white (RGB 255, 255, 255).
🌐 Real-Time English/Chinese Swapping: Perfect for global cross-border sellers and agencies running audits for international clients. Switch the entire dashboard interface, gauges, metrics, and suggestions instantly between English and Chinese with a single click.
🤝 Trust & Rating Analyzer: Evaluates your rating scores and review thresholds. Warns you of conversion blocks (e.g., ratings below 4.5 or sparse reviews under 15).
💡 Smart Actionable Suggestions: Clear, ranked recommendations divided by impact levels (Critical, Medium, Info) to help you fix errors instantly.
Why Choose This Tool?
100% Non-Invasive & Safe: Runs entirely client-side. No Seller Central API keys or Amazon logins required.
Global Support: Fully compatible across all 15+ global Amazon marketplaces (e.g., .com, .co.uk, .de, .cn, .co.jp, .ca, .in, etc.).
Category Smart: Detects and alters scoring weights between Consumer Electronics and Standard Physical Goods automatically.
3. Store Detailed Description (Chinese 简体中文)
一键诊断亚马逊 Listing 健康度与 CDQ 数据质量，抢占流量先机！
多数亚马逊 FBA 卖家因为 Listing 信息不全或不合规，导致自然搜索排名下降、广告转化率低下。“亚马逊 Listing 健康度与 CDQ 评分分析助手”是一款完全免费、轻量且强大的谷歌浏览器插件。它能帮助卖家、出海代运营团队和审计机构一键对全球任意亚马逊产品详情页进行深度合规性体检。

核心功能亮点：
🏆 即时健康度跑分 (0-100)： 根据亚马逊官方最新 Listing 推荐标准进行动态权重打分，快速识别 Listing 短板。
🛡️ CDQ (综合数据质量评分) 深度审计：
亚马逊极其重视 CDQ 评分，它决定了您的 ASIN 是否有资格申报 Prime Day 会员日秒杀、Lightning Deals (秒杀)、Best Deals等高转化活动。
插件自动诊断：基础属性填写、5点描述丰富度、黄金核心属性（材质、容量、尺寸、重量、颜色）及品类特有关键属性（电子产品接口、功率、型号；服饰类尺码表、适用人群）。
📝 2025 标题重复字词新规规避： 亚马逊自 2025 年 1 月 21 日起对标题中单个字词/词组的重复次数限制在 2 次以内（否则将面临系统降权或 14 定整改）。插件自动分析标题重复率，助您完美避坑。
🔍 主图缩放 (Main Image Zoom) 与纯白检测：
自动还原亚马逊 CDN 主图的原始母图分辨率，检测最长边是否达到 1,600px（激活 hover 缩放悬停功能的硬性要求）。
运用 HTML5 离线 Canvas 像素取样，智能分析主图背景是否为纯白色 (RGB 255, 255, 255)，避免被系统扣分。
🌐 中英文一键无缝热切换： 专为跨境电商卖家、跨境代运营、出海顾问机构设计。支持在弹出窗口一键切换 EN / 中文。无论是为本地团队操作还是为海外客户演示Listing审计报告，均能秒级呈现。
🤝 信任度度量： 统计分析 Review 评论数与 Star Rating 星级评分。针对转化痛点（如评级低于 4.5 或评论数不足 15 条）发出警示。
💡 智能优化建议清单： 按优先级（致命缺陷、改进建议、温馨提示）分组列出改进动作，并指导如何在后台进行修改。
